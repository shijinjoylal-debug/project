Heizenberg's Uncertainty Principle
Δx⋅Δp≥ 2/ℏ
where: Δx is the uncertainty in position,
Δp is the uncertainty in momentum,
ℏ (h-bar) is the reduced Planck constant, approximately equal to \(1.054 \times 10\^{-34} \text{ Js}\).
